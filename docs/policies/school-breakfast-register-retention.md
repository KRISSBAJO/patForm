# School Breakfast Register retention policy (pilot)

**Owner:** School Office. **Scope:** The Patform `brekfast_list` process, including pupil names, classes, meal type, review notes, and any associated evidence. This is an internal pilot policy, not a statement of a statutory minimum. The school must check whether a funding programme, audit requirement, safeguarding matter, or its wider records schedule requires a different period before entering real pupil data.

1. Canteen Staff enter one row for each pupil who ate breakfast. Only signed-in Canteen Staff may start the form. The School Office reviews the register and may return it for correction before recording it.
2. A register in the **Recorded** or **Cancelled** state is retained for **90 days after completion**. An active or returned register is outside this clock. Access remains limited to authorized school roles while it is held.
3. At least monthly, an authorized administrator previews the retention pass for `brekfast_list`, reviews the count and any applicable hold, and then applies the pass. The Patform setting `intent.retentionDays = 90` controls which completed records are eligible. Setting the period alone does **not** schedule deletion. The retention command removes eligible records and their history and leaves an audit entry for the deletion.
4. If a record must be preserved for a documented investigation, complaint, funding review, or legal duty, the School Office must arrange the hold before the deletion pass. The current process has no per-record retention hold; the administrator must pause the pass for this process until that need is resolved.
5. The School Office reviews this policy before live use and at least annually, and updates the published process if its approved schedule changes. Backup expiry and exports need separate operational controls; the process retention setting does not erase copies outside the live record store.

This 90-day period is a proposed pilot choice based on the current form's operational purpose. The [ICO storage limitation guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/) requires a justified period and regular review. [DfE school census guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/complete-the-school-census/data-items-2026-to-2027) includes breakfast club attendance requirements for participating schools from autumn 2026; the school must account for those requirements before using this policy for real pupils.
